Keep this attribution and the full manifest provenance with redistributed audio;

No publication is performed by this development step.

Models were downloaded at the exact repository revision in manifest.json and
verified against upstream LFS SHA-256 values. MODEL_CARD and config SHA values,
download URLs, and source-WAV/encoded/text hashes are recorded in the manifest.

Transformations: offline synthesis at length_scale 1.05; 22,050 Hz mono to
16,000 Hz signed little-endian PCM using Python 3.12 audioop.ratecv(1,1);
long outside silence trimmed with 120 ms margins; block IMA-ADPCM compression.
WAVs are retained for comparison; all normal firmware clips use IMA.
Synthesis can vary between runs; the accepted bytes are SHA-bound.
Format: 32-byte LPA2 header, mono/16-bit/16 kHz, header and payload CRC32.
IMA: 256-byte blocks, 505 samples/block, predictor and step index per block.
Reference PCM for the first hotel sentence is embedded in the same pack.
All playback streams through the original BSP PCM interface.
